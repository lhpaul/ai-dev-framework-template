- **Readiness check 0.5 no longer misreads large loop summaries** (#2229): the Protocol 91 Step 8a readiness checklist now passes the reviewer-loop summary to `grep` through a here-string instead of `echo ... | grep -q`. Under `pipefail`, a summary larger than the pipe buffer made `echo` die of SIGPIPE, so check 0.5 blocked clean summaries as "not clean" and check 0.6 could miss a skipped result. A regression test runs both checks against a ~1 MB summary.

- **Capacity retries share the round budget** (#2151): `local-ai-reviewer.sh` no longer gives every provider-overload retry a full `--timeout`. The first attempt always gets the full budget, each retry runs with only the time left in the round, and when the backoff plus another attempt no longer fit it ends as `needs_rerun` / `provider_overloaded`, so backoff and retries cannot stretch a round to about 3x the requested budget.

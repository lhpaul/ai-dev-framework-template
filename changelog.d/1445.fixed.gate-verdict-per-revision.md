- **Internal review gate verdicts now bind to the reviewed commit, not the branch** (#1445): a PR could previously rewrite substantial sections after the Step 7a internal review gate approved, then treat a clean automated-reviewer-loop run at the new HEAD as sufficient — the gate itself was never re-run on the second revision. `REVIEW.md` and protocols 01/02/03 now state the binding rule explicitly, the Step 7a summary comment now records a required `**Gate-approved commit**` SHA, and a new Step 8a readiness check runs `internal-review-gate-freshness-guard.sh` to refuse readiness when that SHA no longer matches the PR's live HEAD, unless the delta is explicitly marked mechanical (`MECHANICAL_DELTA:` on every commit in range, <=10 changed lines).

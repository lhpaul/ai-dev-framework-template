- **Plan outcome record may lag the PR head by one stamp-only push** (#1820): the plan-authoring rigor rules required the per-rule outcome record's `Plan revision:` to equal the plan PR head exactly. A commit cannot name its own SHA, and a head the local reviewer already refused (`failed_for_head`) needs a new push before it can be reviewed again, so the record could never catch up and the loop deadlocked. `plan-authoring-rigor-rules.md` now defines once when the stated revision is current: the head itself, or the head's only parent when the head is not a merge commit and nothing changed between them except the `Plan revision:` SHA (or nothing at all). A stamp two or more commits behind, a merge head, or any other change since the stamped revision still fails. The plan reviewer agents and Protocol 02 point to that definition, and the new read-only `outcome-record-revision-check.sh` applies it with `git` alone. Regression coverage in `tests/test-outcome-record-revision-check.sh`.

- **Advisory checks tests no longer pin the shipped no-op body** (#2168): `test-run-advisory-checks.sh` and the advisory-hook area of `test-pr-review-loop.sh` now check the caller contract everywhere (a PR number is required, a missing or empty one fails with usage on stderr, and the caller sees only the script's stdout) and check that the shipped `run-advisory-checks.sh` prints nothing only in the template itself. A project that replaces the hook body with its own advisory checks, as the hook invites, can keep both suites after a template sync instead of declining them.

- **Timed-out local AI reviews no longer leave their review copy behind** (#2172):
  the `local-ai-reviewer.sh` watchdog now gives its TERM grace period to the
  whole process group, not only its leader. On hosts where `sh -c` does not
  exec the command (dash, including Ubuntu CI runners), the leader died at
  once and the group was killed while the bundled Claude preset was still
  removing its `local-claude-review.*` copy. The `T-B13` leftover check now
  runs as soon as the reviewer returns instead of after a fixed 3-second sleep.

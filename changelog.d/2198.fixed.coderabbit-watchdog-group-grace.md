- **Timed-out CodeRabbit CLI reviews no longer cut off their own cleanup**
  (#2198): the `coderabbit-cli-reviewer.sh` watchdog now gives its TERM grace
  period to the whole process group, not only its leader, as #2172 did for
  `local-ai-reviewer.sh`. On hosts where `sh -c` does not exec the command
  (dash, including Ubuntu CI runners), the leader died at once and the group
  was killed while descendants were still running their TERM/EXIT cleanup.
